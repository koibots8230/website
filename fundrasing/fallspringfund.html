Fundraising

Why It's Important

Fundraising is essential to keep our robotics team thriving. Building robots and maintaining the team is a significant financial challenge. Last season, we spent over $35,000, and our goal this fall is to raise enough to ensure a strong foundation for next year. Your donations directly support our goal, providing a free, fun, hands-on STEM experience for dozens of students each year.

What You Get

4 kids come to your house and do yard work (weeding, raking mulching, lawn mowing, pressure washing etc)

Price

Minimum donation: $275
Higher amounts are welcome!

Location

Anwhere in Arlington, Va or very close by
Our yard jobs are booked in four-hour timeslots. If you need a larger job done, you can book multiple, but do be advised that we expect separate and full payments for each slot booked.
